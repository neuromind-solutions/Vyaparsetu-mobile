VyapaarSetu (व्यापार सेतु)

A dedicated desktop and web accounting system built for Indian agricultural produce commission agents (आढ़ती / Vyapari) and wholesale traders to streamline daily farmer purchase, trader billing, and ledger management.

⚡ Fast Offline-First SQLite 🔒 Encrypted Google Drive Backups 📊 Double-Entry Khata Reconciliation 🧾 WhatsApp PDF Invoicing

Purpose of Application

VyapaarSetu bridges the gap between traditional agricultural bookkeeping and modern, error-free accounting.

🌾

Farmer (Kisan) Purchase & Patti

Record daily farm arrivals, crate weights, mandi deductions (palledari, cess), and automatically generate instant farmer payment slips (Patti).

🛒

Buyer (Vyapari) Sales & Invoicing

Create accurate sales invoices with customized commission rates, tax breakdowns, crate deposit tracking, and direct WhatsApp sharing.

📒

Khata & Double-Entry Ledgers

Maintain accurate cash, bank, farmer, and buyer accounts with real-time balance calculations, payment receipts, and payment aging reports.

Google OAuth 2.0 & Google Drive Integration

Transparent explanation of why and how VyapaarSetu accesses Google APIs.

VyapaarSetu uses Google OAuth 2.0 strictly to provide business owners with an automated, encrypted cloud backup and disaster recovery mechanism for their local database records.

  • ✓
    Restricted App-Specific Scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file):
    VyapaarSetu requests access only to view, upload, and update the specific database backup files and folders created by VyapaarSetu (under the dedicated MandaiMitra_Backups folder).
  • ✓
    Zero Access to Other Drive Files:
    VyapaarSetu has no permission or ability to view, read, modify, or delete any personal documents, photos, spreadsheets, or files stored in your Google Drive.
  • ✓
    User-Controlled Data Storage:
    All backup snapshots are stored strictly within the user's personal Google Drive account. We operate no external central server storing your financial or transaction data.
  • ✓
    One-Click Disconnect & Revocation:
    Users can disconnect their Google Drive account at any time from the app Settings or through their Google Account Security Settings.

VyapaarSetu complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
